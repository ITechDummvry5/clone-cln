Got Questions?

Frequently asked

Can I cancel or change my booking?

Yes. Most packages offer free cancellation up to 30 days before departure. Specific terms vary by package and are clearly shown before checkout.

Do you offer travel insurance?

Optional insurance plans are available at checkout. We partner with leading providers to cover trip cancellation, medical emergencies, and lost luggage.

How do I get support while travelling?

Your dedicated concierge is reachable 24/7 via phone, WhatsApp, or in-app chat. Emergency contacts are provided in your digital travel pack before departure.

Are flights included in packages?

Packages are marked clearly as flight-inclusive or land-only. You can also customise any package to add or remove flights at the point of booking.

Can I build a custom itinerary?

Absolutely. Our bespoke team can design a trip from scratch around your dates, interests, and budget. Contact us to get started.
