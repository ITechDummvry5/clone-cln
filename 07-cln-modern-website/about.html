Design Philosophy
Acoustics First

Sound is the product's
first impression.

Before a user sees the keyboard, they hear it. We use computational fluid dynamics to simulate and tune the internal sound bar cavity, redirecting every actuation's energy back through the keycap rather than outward into the room.

The result is the signature TOPKEYS "thock" — a deep, low-frequency resonance that feels as intentional as the snap of a high-end camera shutter.

Material Honesty

If it touches your hand,
it must be extraordinary.

The case is CNC-milled from a single billet of 6063 aluminum, then sandblasted to a precise 20-micron matte texture. The quartz glass rotary encoder is optically polished — not to be luxurious, but because optical-grade clarity is the only standard worth meeting.

We chose brass for the plate not because it is fashionable, but because its acoustic mass properties are mathematically superior for sub-200Hz resonance amplification.

Software as Hardware

The firmware is as
refined as the chassis.

Veo runs a custom QMK fork we call VeoOS. Every layer, macro, and RGB state is compiled locally and never touches a cloud server. Your configuration is stored in 128KB of onboard EEPROM flash — yours permanently, no app required.

Per-key polling happens at 8000Hz. Not because anyone needs it to, but because precision at that level changes how a board feels under latency-sensitive workloads.

How We Build

Every Veo is made-to-order.
Every detail is deliberate.

01
Configuration
You specify. We listen.

Your case color, plate material, and switch preference are logged at the moment of reservation. No generic batch production — each build is assembled against a specific spec sheet with your name on it.

02
Machining
CNC milled from billet.

Your case is cut from a fresh 6063 aluminum block. The process takes 4.5 hours per unit. We don't speed it up. The final surface is hand-checked for any tooling marks before proceeding.

03
Assembly
Layer by layer, by hand.

IXPE sheet is cut to spec. Poron gaskets are seated. The PCB is checked under UV for trace continuity. Switches are hand-lubed a second time at this stage before installation.

04
Acoustic QC
Every key must pass the test.

Each completed unit is run through an automated acoustic analysis rig. All 87 keys are individually actuated and measured. Any key outside our -18dB frequency tolerance is replaced before shipping.
